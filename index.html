About Kenya

Kenya is a country in East Africa with a coastline on the Indian Ocean. It encompasses savannah, lakelands, the dramatic Great Rift Valley and mountain highlands. It's also home to wildlife like lions, elephants and rhinos. From the Maasai Mara National Reserve, known for its annual wildebeest migration, to the bustling capital of Nairobi, Kenya offers a diverse range of experiences for travelers.
